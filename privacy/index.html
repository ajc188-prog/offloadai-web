Privacy Policy for OffloadAI

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Core Privacy Principle: 100% On-Device Processing

OffloadAI is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. All photo scanning, metadata analysis (creation dates, EXIF coordinates), spatial-temporal clustering, and natural language processing (powered by Apple's on-device FoundationModels and NaturalLanguage frameworks) occur exclusively on your iOS device. We do not operate any cloud servers for photo processing, and your photos, videos, or personal library data are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored externally by us.

2. Photo Library & External Storage Access

  • Photo Library (PhotoKit): Used solely to read asset metadata for local folder organization, stream original assets during user-initiated backups, and move backed-up assets to the iOS "Recently Deleted" album upon your explicit confirmation.
  • External Storage (FileManager): Used solely to write your selected photos and folder structures to the external USB-C drive or local directory you explicitly authorize via the iOS Document Picker.

3. Location Data & Apple MapKit

OffloadAI does not track your real-time physical location. To resolve GPS coordinates embedded in your photos into readable landmark or venue names, the app sends anonymous coordinate queries to Apple MapKit APIs (MKLocalSearch and MKReverseGeocodingRequest). When offline, the app relies on a bundled, read-only local spatial database.

4. Speech Recognition & Microphone

When you use the optional voice input feature to search or organize photos, audio is processed via Apple's native SFSpeechRecognizer and AVAudioRecorder for visual audio-level feedback. No voice recordings are saved or transmitted to us.

5. In-App Purchases & Quota Sync

All transactions are processed securely through Apple StoreKit 2. We do not have access to your credit card or billing details. To preserve your free trial quota and Pro entitlement status across app reinstalls, non-identifiable quota state flags are stored locally in the iOS Keychain, synchronized via your personal iCloud Key-Value Store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore), and recorded as a metadata file (.offload_quota_seal.json) inside your backup directory.

6. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

OffloadAI contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics frameworks.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@offloadai.space

OffloadAI 私隱政策
